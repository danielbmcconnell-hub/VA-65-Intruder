# Milestone 2.2 play guide

Open the game through its deployed website, then choose a starting point in the **Ready Room**. Use landscape orientation on iPhone. Start with Medium graphics, or Low if movement feels slow.

## Choose a chapter

| Ready Room button | Experience |
| --- | --- |
| **Enter the prison** | Original physical cell, compound, rooftop, Hanoi and river escape. |
| **Solitary survival** | Standalone practice in confinement, remembered projects and communication. |
| **Second escape** | Standalone fictional practice in the regular compound, with two physical escape approaches. |
| **Red River journey** | Standalone swimming, current, cover and evasion practice. |
| **Continue POW checkpoint** | Resume your latest unfinished local checkpoint. |

**Saved chapters** offers separate **Resume** buttons for chapters you have saved. Standalone practice does not award campaign qualifications or complete captivity objectives for you. To progress through the full imprisonment storyline, continue the original escape or resume its campaign checkpoint.

## Move, look and interact

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Touchscreen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | W/S or Up/Down arrows | Left stick |
| Turn / look | A/D or Left/Right arrows; Q/E looks vertically; Y enables mouse look | Drag the right side |
| Move quietly | Hold C to crouch | Crouch control |
| Run | Hold Shift | Run control |
| Environmental work | Face the object and hold F until progress finishes; release before another action | Face the object and hold **Interact**; release between actions |
| Climb / dive | Space at an appropriate object / in the river | **Climb** / **Dive** |

The short tutorial teaches these actions through movement. **Skip tutorial** dismisses it; **Settings & controls → Controls tutorial** re-enables guidance. The center reticle helps you face a target. A prompt appears only when work is within reach. Turning toward another object can change that prompt.

Expand **Condition** for body, fatigue, resilience, morale, memory and hope. **Guard sound / sight cues** in Settings adds directional, contextual hints. Guard speech is reconstructed game dialogue. A solid wall can block sight while a loud sound still brings investigation. Running, illicit hardware work and entering restricted areas draw attention; calm movement in the permitted yard is allowed. Escape from a pursuit requires breaking sight and allowing the search to fade.

Use **Save** before an uncertain attempt. Saves belong to this website and browser/device; they do not synchronize between Windows and iPhone.

## Discover the second escape

Begin by inspecting the door slot in your regular cell. Observation explains the supervised **16:00–18:00** yard period and the two possible routes. During ordinary play, one real second represents one simulated minute here. Rest on the mat advances eight simulated hours over sixteen real seconds; moving interrupts rest.

The western washing passage requires a cloth-wrapped tool and more careful work. Its screen and continuous walls provide physical cover. The eastern workshop is more exposed; a quick forced hinge makes a loud metallic sound and causes guards to investigate. The existing three-fastener workshop option is still available. Patrol timing can change which route takes longer overall.

Watch actual movement, listen at the workshop wall, and check the prompt before committing to work. Opening a hatch or grate does not finish the escape: walk through the passage and beyond the compound boundary. Both routes continue into Hanoi and the existing river journey. They do not grant automatic rescue. Capture returns you to confinement while preserving remembered projects and contacts.

## Optional captivity pacing

At the bed or **Rest / time**, **Continue established routine** becomes available after two different completed activities, a counted incoming tap message and reply, food, two simulated hours of rest, and a corridor observation. The panel lists unfinished requirements.

An established routine compresses uneventful days at one day per real second. It pauses for changed guard routines, messages, discoveries, interviews and opportunities. Choose **Review this opportunity**, handle it through the relevant physical interaction or communication activity, then **Resume established routine**. **Keep the opportunity for later** is available for suitable events; **Pause the routine** stops compression. It grants no puzzle answers, decoded clues or escape success. Return to regular imprisonment still requires the full storyline's earned objectives.

## A-6A and B/N practice

Choose **Ready Room → Settings → Your seat → Right — B/N**, then return to operations and select **DIANE practice with guidance** or **Carrier practice with guidance**. The original pilot handles flying in B/N mode; you navigate and designate. The practice missions retain their existing hazards, including light AAA in DIANE practice.

Use **SCOPE/Tab** for radar; **X** cycles contacts and arrows fine-slew on desktop, or drag the crosshairs on touch. **LOCK/Enter** designates. **STEER/C** sends the existing steering instruction. Hold **COMMIT/Space** through a valid attack solution when cleared; the computer determines release timing. Monitor the chart, radar altitude and recovery indications.

**Flight guidance: ON/OFF** toggles authored coaching. It describes existing instruments and recorded flight behavior; it does not fly the aircraft or alter its physics or grade. The debrief adds practical observations from the intervals when guidance was enabled.

## History and device limits

The Coker–McKnight escape inspiration remains **October 12, 1967** and ended in recapture. The compound layouts, subsequent escapes, dialogue and rescue outcomes are fictional. Denton, Stockdale, the photographs and the separated-cell communication chapter remain available through **History & photos** and the [Milestone 2.1 guide](MILESTONE_2_1_PLAY_GUIDE.md).

Cloud verification uses actual Chromium WebGL2, desktop layout and simulated iPhone touch input. Physical Windows/iPhone Safari performance and human difficulty balance still need your trial. Keep the previous Netlify deployment available while comparing this build.
