# Prison and ration visual references

The latest supplied cell photograph guides a rebuilt initial escape cell: a narrow central aisle between two raised concrete sleeping platforms, ankle fittings, mottled brown-gray plaster, triangular recessed vents, a tall steel door with a closed inspection flap and a physically arched barred transom. The platforms and doorway have matching collision geometry; the bunk, concealed bracket, tap contact and door-fitting interactions follow the new proportions. The corridor, stairs and rooftop escape route remain reachable. Other prison scenes retain their existing layouts, including the separate three-by-nine-foot solitary cell. This is a playable adaptation of the photograph, not an architectural survey.

Initial capture includes a short, reconstructed bamboo holding scene before the existing prison transfer. The bamboo reference, metal pit cages and interwoven barbed-wire replicas are different constructions. The uploaded `IMG_8215.AVIF` visibly identifies a museum replica of barbed-wire cages; it is preserved unchanged in the private reference folder. Its location, period and relationship to American aviators have not been established here. The game does not combine that exhibit or its museum mannequins into a claimed historical capture event.

The food reference informs an oblong worn tin, rice, greens and a spoon. Complete matching full and empty images provide the optional inspection view and a photographic surface at the existing tray position. The surface has limited depth and perspective; it is not a volumetric reconstruction. Captivity and regular confinement retain their original meal eligibility, daily limits, condition effects and saved state. The picture establishes appearance for this reconstruction; it does not establish every prisoner's diet, portion, calories or routine.

The new original generated material and ration PNGs remain unchanged in `assets/prison/`. Runtime WebPs decode to identical RGBA pixels. The material atlas is cropped only during GPU upload into separate repeating plaster and steel textures so their mipmaps cannot bleed together. Source dimensions, hashes, alpha coverage and encoding are recorded in [the provenance file](../assets/prison/provenance.json).

The ration inspection has Close, Escape and backdrop dismissal. Eating calls the existing meal action; closing the view does not consume food. The simulation waits while that view is open. Capture captions can be hidden and shown, and native Pause freezes the staged transfer.
