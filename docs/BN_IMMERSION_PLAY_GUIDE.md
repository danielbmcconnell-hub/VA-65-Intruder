# B/N Immersion — play guide

This complete build retains all twelve missions, the original A-6A flight model, the McConnell tribute and the earlier survival/POW chapters. The new work concentrates on target correlation, readable combat and Willard's night route.

## Work the target

For an ordinary strike, choose **Right — B/N** in Ready Room Settings. Willard Egress keeps its manual pilot start and B/N guidance; stabilize the aircraft before working the scope.

1. Open **B/N worksheet**, then **SCOPE** (Tab on desktop). The target-area chart shows the briefing circle, available route/offset references and authored landmarks. Its ownship position follows the existing navigation system and its error.
2. Tap the chart to mark a **CANDIDATE**. Independently slew the actual radar with a drag or arrow keys. Compare the briefed layout and return shape; the existing X slew aid remains available.
3. Press **COMPARE ECHO**. Read the measured separation and sweep freshness. A matching position is a working hypothesis; stale or dark returns provide no identification, and a blind coordinate mission remains blind.
4. Choose **RUN-IN**, then tap an initial point on the chart. The worksheet shows the planned course, heading difference and cross-track displacement. This is your advisory plot; it does not fly or designate for you.
5. Use the original **LOCK**, confirm clearance and the steering solution, then hold **COMMIT / Space** through the original ASQ-61 release. A mistaken designation can still miss. Recce retains weapons-tight rules, offset attacks retain their reference procedure, and Iron Hand retains its A-6B ARM system.

The debrief records chart comparisons and the last measured separation without awarding an identification bonus. Chart marks belong to the current flight; they are not additional campaign checkpoints.

## Survive Willard Egress

Choose **Ready Room → 12 · WILLARD EGRESS → PLAY MISSION**. Existing strike/gun-belt checkpoints and saved Continue remain available.

- **H / LOOK AFT** checks behind. While looking aft, **Z / REAR ZOOM** magnifies the actual perspective view from 54° to 34°. The enemy stays at its actual world position and size. Forward view and **V / VIEW** clear magnification.
- Brief alerts distinguish actual cannon fire, nearby flak and aircraft hits. Contact direction uses recent crew sightings; it is not a hidden-position tracker. Alerts expire and have cooldowns.
- Follow the B/N through controlled defensive flight, the hostile gun belt and the coastal escape. Both aircraft remain vulnerable to real shells. Breaking contact is followed by the existing playable egress and optional carrier recovery.

## See the night route

Adjust **Night visibility** in the Willard briefing or pause menu. The preference is saved in this browser. Begin near the default 55%, then adjust so terrain and obstacles remain visible on your screen.

The optional strike gains sidings, wagons, sheds and a road junction. Farther along the route, look for the bridge over the actual generated river channel, its hamlet, the twin-stack industrial works and coastal fishing lights. These are authored pilotage landmarks on the game's terrain, not surveyed historical locations or extra scored targets.

Moonlit terrain and water, warm lamps and short illumination from actual flak bursts help separate threats. Low/Medium graphics reduce scenery distance/detail and light counts. Actual iPhone Safari performance still needs device testing.

## Deploy and verify

Download `Intruder_Yankee_Station_BN_Immersion.zip`, choose **Extract All** on Windows and upload the extracted folder containing `index.html`, `assets/` and `_headers` to your existing Netlify project's **Deploys** page. Keep the same origin to retain local saves. The page title identifies **B/N Immersion**; refresh with Ctrl+F5 if necessary without clearing site data.

The live Netlify site is not updated by a Git/file delivery. See the [test and deployment report](BN_IMMERSION_REPORT.md) for actual verification, source identifiers and limitations.
