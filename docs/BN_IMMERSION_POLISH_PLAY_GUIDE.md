# B/N Immersion & Visual Polish — play guide

Use **SHORT B/N PRACTICE** for the existing airborne introduction, or choose Mission 02, **Break-in on Dixie Station**, from the Ready Room. Ordinary strikes also support **Right — B/N** in Settings.

1. Open **B/N worksheet** and cycle **ZOOM ×1/×2/×4**. Tap a published landmark such as the **Banded water tower**, then tap your candidate. Read the bearing and yards from that reference.
2. Open **SCOPE** (Tab on desktop), slew the real radar independently and choose **COMPARE ECHO**. Freshness and separation help assess your judgment; a dark or matching return does not establish identity.
3. Plot **RUN-IN** if useful, then use the original **LOCK** and clearance procedure. Close the worksheet to see the live steering needle, consent state and ASQ countdown. Hold **COMMIT / Space** through the original release when cleared. The strip does not fly or release for you.
4. Observe the strike when safe. Fuel and ammunition aftermath follows actual destruction and secondary events. Then follow the existing egress and return guidance.

In the existing ground/POW chapters, watch patrol steps, alert readiness and hit reactions. These animate the preserved photographic cutouts. Nearby terrain contact improves after a short streamed warm-up; distant ground, shores and steep slopes retain limitations. Enable sound/voices through the normal controls to hear hit transients and speech focus. Device listening and Safari trials are still needed.

On Windows, download the complete ZIP from the private repository while signed in, choose **Extract All**, then upload the extracted folder whose immediate children are `index.html`, `assets/` and `_headers` to the existing Netlify project's **Deploys** page. Confirm the browser tab says **B/N Immersion & Visual Polish**. Keep the same site origin for local saves. Git/file delivery does not update the live site; see the [report](BN_IMMERSION_POLISH_REPORT.md).
