# Generic neural game dialogue

These fictional performances were synthesized locally with Kokoro-82M (Apache2.0) through kokoro-onnx (MIT). They do not reproduce a historical speaker or a private family voice. No game text or audio was sent to a speech service. Model weights and generation dependencies are excluded from the runtime.

Common fixed English calls use three consistent generic American male roles. Selected family-expression openings can be performed ahead of a dynamic caption; remaining dynamic lines use a suitable installed voice or remain captions. Vietnamese keeps its own language.

The upstream license texts are preserved beside this notice. Source WAVs, transcripts, exact source/runtime hashes and generation details remain in the source repository. See manifest.json and provenance.json.
