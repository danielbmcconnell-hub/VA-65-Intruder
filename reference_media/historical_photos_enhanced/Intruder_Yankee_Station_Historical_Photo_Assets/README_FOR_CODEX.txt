INTRUDER: YANKEE STATION — HISTORICAL PHOTO ASSETS

Six separate archival images, upscaled and contrast-corrected for use in the game.
The May 1966 Coker portrait and the Hanoi Hilton aerial are *interpretive* colorizations.
Their original monochrome image geometry, captions, and facial features have been preserved;
color details are estimated and should not be presented as historically certain.

Each filename has a PNG master and smaller WebP web-ready counterpart.
Replace existing assets in the game with the relevant WebP files, update asset mappings,
and ensure HTML image display uses opacity:1, filter:none, mix-blend-mode:normal,
object-fit:contain, and does not crop heads/faces or captions.

WARNING: The original May 1966 photo is approximately 200x218 pixels, so enlargement
cannot recover details that were never present. Other original low-resolution images
have the same limitation. Avoid claiming that upscaled interpretations reveal new history.

Credits and rights: verify image rights/attribution before publishing publicly.
The supplied POW photographs may depict staged scenes or film stills; do not relabel
people or claim provenance not established by their source documentation.
